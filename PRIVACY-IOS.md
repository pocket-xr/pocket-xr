# Pocket XR for iPhone and iPad: privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers **Pocket XR** for iPhone and iPad (`com.hayeco.pocketxr.ios`), published by Haye & Co (Mike Haye).

## The short version

Pocket XR collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting services.
Nothing it reads on your phone is sent to Haye & Co or to anyone else.

## What Pocket XR uses on the phone

Everything below is used on the phone only, while you use the feature.

- **Your XR glasses**: as a screen, and, where iOS allows it, their motion sensor over their USB cable for head
  tracking (this is the reason for the Local Network permission).
- **Local Network**: to reach your glasses over their USB cable, and to find and stream from your own computer for PC
  screens and PC VR (Pocket XR on your computer, Apollo, Sunshine or ALVR). The connection goes only to the computer you
  choose, and pictures, sound and your input travel between your phone and that computer only.
- **The phone's motion sensor**, for the Spatial Mouse (pointing the phone), only while it is on.
- **Game controllers, mice and keyboards** you connect, as input.
- **Photos (add only)**: to save recordings you make with Record. Pocket XR never reads your photo library.

## What is saved on the phone

- Your settings, windows, layouts and the computers you paired, in Pocket XR's own storage. A key and certificate
  that identify this phone to your computers, in the phone's keychain.
- Web pages you open in windows are handled by Apple's WebKit, which keeps its own cookies and cache in Pocket XR's
  storage, as Safari would.
- A short log of Pocket XR's own messages, kept on the phone; it leaves only if you share a report yourself.
- Recordings, saved to Photos when you stop recording.

## What goes over the internet

- Web pages you open, from the sites you choose.
- Nothing else: Pocket XR has no servers. Updates come from the App Store.
