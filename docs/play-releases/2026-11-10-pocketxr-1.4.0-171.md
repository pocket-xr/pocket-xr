# Pocket XR 1.4.0 (171): closed testing release, 2026-11-10

Status: **committed to Closed testing - Alpha** through the API, replacing 1.3.4 (170), which was in review. The edit
was committed without `changesNotSentForReview`, so Play takes it for review itself; check Publishing overview in Play
Console. Production and the store listing were not touched.

Why now: 1.4.0 adds Steady mode in 6DoF on the XREAL Aura (GitHub release v1.4.0 the same day), and Mike wanted Play
testers to have it too.
