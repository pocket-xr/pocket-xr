# Pocket XR 1.5.1 (181): closed testing release, 2026-11-15

Status: **committed to Closed testing - Alpha** through the API, replacing 1.4.7 (179). The edit was committed without
`changesNotSentForReview`, so Play takes it for review itself; check Publishing overview in Play Console. Production
and the store listing were not touched.

Why now: 1.5.0 and 1.5.1 (GitHub releases v1.5.0 and v1.5.1 the same day) bring the tour, a two-minute tutorial shown
once to new installs and open at any time from the Menu and Pocket XR Settings, and its translation into the ten other
languages. 1.5.0 (180) had no Play release: the testers go from 1.4.7 straight to 1.5.1, so they get the tour already
translated. Someone updating is never shown the tour by itself; the trackpad offers it once.
