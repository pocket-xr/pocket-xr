# Pocket XR 1.4.4 (175): closed testing release, 2026-11-12

Status: **committed to Closed testing - Alpha** through the API, replacing 1.4.3 (174). The edit was committed without
`changesNotSentForReview`, so Play takes it for review itself; check Publishing overview in Play Console. Production
and the store listing were not touched.

Why now: 1.4.4 (GitHub release v1.4.4 the same day) shows Pocket XR in the phone's language: English, Korean, Japanese,
Chinese (simplified and traditional), Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian and Russian. A Reddit user
asked for Korean. Checked in Korean and German on an emulator, and the signed GitHub build runs on Mike's phone.
