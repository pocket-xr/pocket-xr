## Links

- **Tester group:** `pocketxr-testers@mikehaye.com`, join page
  https://groups.google.com/a/mikehaye.com/g/pocketxr-testers
  A Google Group in the mikehaye.com Workspace (owner connect@mikehaye.com), not googlegroups.com. External members
  are allowed, so personal Gmail accounts can join. Settings: anyone on the web can see the group and join without
  approval, only group managers can see members, only group owners can post. It is the tester list on the track's
  Testers tab under Google Groups.
- **Play opt-in link:** https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.hayeco.flipspaces
  It works once a release is rolled out to the closed track. A tester must join the group first, using the same
  Google account as on their phone.
